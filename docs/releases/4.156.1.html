Highlights

SkiaSharp 4.156.1 corrects GPU geometry, clip recording and displacement-filter precision without changing managed APIs.

Breaking Changes

None in this release.

Bug Fixes

  • Transformed shapes and GPU bookkeeping — Corrects ellipse and rounded-rectangle axis mapping and clamps transformed corner radii to prevent overlap. Empty Ganesh flushes no longer leave stale atlas tokens. Graphite accounts for unrecorded clips and warns and skips draws whose clip stack cannot fit a fresh pass. (#5687)
  • Displacement precision and Vulkan waits — Displacement offsets now use float precision to match filter bounds. Native synchronous Vulkan submission incorporates fence-wait failure while preserving timeout retries and asynchronous behavior; GRContext.Submit remains void and gains no managed failure result or exception. (#5711)
