## `SKData.CreateCopy` length validation (#5429)

`SKData.CreateCopy(byte[], ulong)` now throws `ArgumentOutOfRangeException` when the requested
length exceeds the array. Pass a length within the source array instead of relying on an
out-of-bounds native read. The merge-commit subject names the internal C-string over-read fix,
but its body does not describe this public validation change.

## Borrowed front-buffered streams are released on disposal (#5327)

Disposing `SKFrontBufferedManagedStream` now clears its managed reference to the wrapped
stream even when `disposeUnderlyingStream` is false, without disposing that borrowed stream.
The caller still owns the borrowed stream's disposal. This fixes retention after disposal;
it does not extend the lifetime of backing data. The merge-commit body contains only
co-author trailers, so its title alone does not establish the ownership behavior.
